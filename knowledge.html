Knowledge portals

two sources of truth · everything here traces back to one of them

This is Continia's own product knowledge, written down so a claim can be checked before it ships. It is built from two portals and nothing else. Docs decides what the product does; continia.com decides how it is said. Where this page and a live page disagree, the live page is right and this page needs fixing.

Verified against both portals on September 23, 2026. Anything dated — end-of-sale, end-of-support, release names, supported Business Central versions, e-invoicing mandates — gets re-read on the live page before it goes into an asset, even when it is written here.

Solutions

nine entries · six active, two legacy, one discontinued
Legacy and discontinued solutions are documented here on purpose: they come up in customer and partner conversations, and the useful answer is the successor plus the migration path, not silence. They never go into new marketing material as current products.

Platform & shared components

the pieces that span several solutions
These are sold as part of a solution rather than on their own, which is why a claim about them has to name the solution it applies to — Secure Archive in Document Capture and Expense Management, the Web Approval Portal in Document Capture and Expense Management, CDN inbound in Document Capture and outbound in Document Output.
